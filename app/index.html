Road to the Platinum — “Vice Got a Price!”

About 200 hours, most of it on the 100% checklist (GTABase, PowerPyx; Push Square’s guide says up to 300+). One playthrough, no difficulty settings, no mission replay — free roam carries on after the story. Cheats switch trophies and saving off, so keep them off your trophy run. Sources: GTABase, Vice City Hub.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story, and side content as it shows up. Play through the five chapters and take on optional missions and character quests as they appear — many are only offered for a while. Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals with binoculars as you go, and turn on progress and collectible notifications. In Chapter 1, do not skip Lucia’s balcony meditation (see Positive Mental Attitude). Steer clear of the Irredeemable criminal profile: it is permanent. GTABase
  2. Step 2 — Finish the 100% checklist. After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work through what is left: every robbery, bank vault and gang operation, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races, Bret’s Target Decks and 70 of the 80 challenges. This is the longest stretch, around 100 hours. GTABase
  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and clean-up. Stranger missions are not part of 100%, but Strangers & Freaks needs 8 strands, and several trophies come from character quest lines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret). Finish those, then check the list for anything left. GTABase

Missable? Only one trophy is possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — a Chapter 1 meditation with Lucia that is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not confirmed yet whether the trophy needs it (GTABase, GGRecon). Everything else can be done after the story. Story missions cannot be replayed, so use your manual save slots before big missions (Vice City Hub).
